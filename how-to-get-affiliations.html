Affiliation Process

4 Simple Steps to Open Your MGITM Center

The MGITM affiliation process is straightforward, transparent, and designed to get your center up and running quickly.

1

Submit Application

Fill the Branch Collaboration Application Form online with your center's details, location, and proposed programs.

2

Document Submission

Submit required documents: ID proof, address proof, institute registration (if existing), and infrastructure details.

3

Verification & Inspection

MGITM's regional team verifies your documents and conducts a center inspection to ensure quality standards are met.

4

Receive Affiliation

After approval, receive your MGITM Center Code, affiliation certificate, study materials, and portal access to begin enrolling students.

Eligibility & Requirements

Minimum Requirements

  • Minimum 200 sq ft dedicated classroom space
  • At least 5 computer workstations with internet access (for IT programs)
  • Qualified instructor with minimum graduate-level education
  • Valid address proof and contact information of center owner
  • Email ID for official communication and portal access

Documents Required

  • Government ID proof of center director/owner (Aadhaar/PAN)
  • Address proof of proposed center location
  • Educational qualification certificates of instructor(s)
  • Photographs of classroom and computer lab (interior)
  • Registration certificate (if existing institute)
