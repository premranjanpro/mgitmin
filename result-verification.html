About This Portal

MGITM Online Result & Certificate Verification

MGITM provides a fully transparent online verification portal for all issued certificates, diplomas, and mark sheets. Employers, universities, and government departments can instantly authenticate the credentials of any MGITM-certified student.

Simply enter the student's Roll / Registration Number to retrieve their academic record, including course name, examination session, marks obtained, and final result status.

Secure & Official

All records are maintained in our secure database and verified against official marksheets issued by MGITM examination board.

24×7 Availability

The result portal is available round-the-clock, allowing instant verification at any time, from anywhere in India.

Printable Statement

Once verified, the academic record can be printed as an official document for submission to employers or universities.
