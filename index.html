Welcome to MGITM

A Premier Technical & Management Education Network

Governed from the capital of India, Mahatma Gandhi Institute of Technology & Management (MGITM) is an ISO 9001:2015 certified autonomous educational institution registered in Delhi NCR with corporate offices across UP, Uttarakhand, Haryana & Punjab.

Our mission is to spread IT literacy and job-ready vocational education at your doorstep — bridging the digital divide for both rural and urban youth across the nation.

Through our Open Session System (OSS), regular degree students can legally pursue skill-based technical diplomas simultaneously — per Supreme Court of India guidelines — to power self-employment and career readiness.
