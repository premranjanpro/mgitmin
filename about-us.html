Who We Are

Mahatma Gandhi Institute of Technology & Management

Mahatma Gandhi Institute of Technology & Management (MGITM) is an ISO 9001:2015 Certified premier educational institution established with the vision of providing world-class technical and vocational education across India. Governed from the capital, Delhi NCR, MGITM has grown into a nationally recognized body with corporate offices across Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Haryana, and Punjab.

Our institute operates on the principle that quality education should reach every corner of India — from metros to rural districts. Through a robust network of 800+ affiliated learning centers, we make job-ready skill education accessible to millions.

MGITM's curriculum spans Computer Science & IT, Teacher Training, Finance & Management, Engineering Trades, Creative Arts, and Health & Care — covering 75+ accredited diploma and certificate programs designed to meet evolving industry needs.

A key pillar of our mission is the Open Session System (OSS) — a Supreme Court approved framework that allows regular university/college students to simultaneously pursue vocational skill certifications, empowering them to graduate with both academic degrees and industry-ready skills.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted technical and vocational education network — nurturing self-reliant, skilled, and entrepreneurially driven citizens who contribute meaningfully to national growth and the global economy.

Our Mission

  • Provide affordable, accessible, and accredited technical education across India
  • Promote digital literacy and IT awareness in rural and semi-urban communities
  • Foster entrepreneurship and self-employment through hands-on skill programs
  • Build a network of quality-assured centers that uphold ISO 9001:2015 standards
