Who we are ?

an ISO 9001:2008 Certified Institute was incorporated with the idea of providing best to best services in the field of professional, vocational, technical courses. The motive of MAHATMA GANDHI INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY AND MANAGEMENT is to show its presence in almost all regions of India with strong network. We have our own offices in 2 states of India i.e., Delhi, UTTAR PRADESH and a network of members in other states. Our motive is to cover almost all geographical regions of INDIA, with its head office based.

Through this network we target members who are active in promoting our study palns at end user level. The motive is to provide best to best knowledge with the advance technology to the students imparting education in any area of the country. Our motive is to aware the youth generation with proficiency in the field of Technicals, Professional and Vocational trades. The main motive is to correlate the rural and urban areas, with modern techniques. Our motive is to provide all the facilities to the students residing in any part of the country. Our focus is considered towards the gals of rural areas with a motive to build up the confidence.
